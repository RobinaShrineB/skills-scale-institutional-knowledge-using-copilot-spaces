# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects design scalable, maintainable technical solutions. They ensure architectural decisions align with long-term strategy, technical standards, and organizational goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical solution architecture and design patterns
- Evaluate technology choices and trade-offs
- Ensure scalability, maintainability, and security of solutions
- Provide technical guidance to development teams
- Document architectural decisions and rationale
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies

### Goals
- Enable teams to build scalable, maintainable systems
- Reduce technical debt and complexity
- Ensure consistency across technology stack and standards

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Partners on implementation feasibility, provides architectural guidance, reviews technical designs
- **With Project Managers**: Advises on technical feasibility and timeline implications of architectural decisions
- **With Product Managers**: Collaborates on technical feasibility of features and long-term scalability concerns

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture decision records (ADRs)
- Design meetings and technical spike documents
- Code review comments on architectural concerns

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads establish testing strategies, define acceptance criteria, and ensure quality standards are met throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards and acceptance criteria
- Establish comprehensive testing strategy (unit, integration, end-to-end, performance)
- Design and maintain test cases and test automation
- Identify and escalate quality risks
- Track and report on quality metrics and test coverage
- Collaborate on bug prioritization and resolution

### Goals
- Ensure products meet quality standards before release
- Reduce defects and improve user experience
- Enable fast, confident releases with regression prevention

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Collaborates on test planning and automation, provides quality feedback, participates in code reviews for testability
- **With Product Managers**: Clarifies acceptance criteria and requirements, ensures feature quality aligns with user expectations
- **With Project Managers**: Reports on quality status and timelines for quality activities

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and quality reports
- Bug tracking and escalation
- Quality metrics dashboards and retrospective feedback

---

## Stakeholder Communications Lead

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Communications Leads manage stakeholder expectations, provide transparent status updates, and ensure timely escalation of blockers and risks.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain stakeholder communication plans
- Provide regular status updates and progress reports
- Manage stakeholder expectations and alignment
- Escalate blockers, risks, and issues appropriately
- Coordinate communication across multiple stakeholders
- Facilitate stakeholder feedback into planning processes

### Goals
- Maintain stakeholder confidence and transparency
- Enable timely decision-making through clear information
- Prevent communication gaps and misalignment

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **With Project Managers**: Reports to PM on communication status, supports status report preparation, helps identify escalation items
- **With Product Managers**: Communicates product direction and feature progress to stakeholders
- **With Development Teams**: Gathers technical status and context to communicate effectively to non-technical stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly/bi-weekly status reports and dashboard updates
- Stakeholder meetings and briefings
- Risk and issue escalations

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release planning, manage deployment timelines, and ensure smooth transitions from development to production.

### Responsibilities
- Develop release plans and deployment schedules
- Manage release dependencies and coordination across teams
- Track release readiness and deployment checklist
- Coordinate deployment activities and rollback procedures
- Monitor release health and production stability
- Document release notes and deployment procedures

### Goals
- Enable predictable, reliable release cycles
- Minimize deployment risk and downtime
- Ensure smooth coordination across multiple teams

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Coordinates on code readiness, merge windows, and deployment procedures
- **With Quality Assurance Leads**: Ensures quality gates are met before release approval
- **With Project Managers**: Tracks release timeline and coordinates with project schedules
- **With Operations/DevOps**: Manages deployment execution and production monitoring

### Typical Communication
- Release plans and deployment checklists
- Release readiness reviews and go/no-go decisions
- Release notes and post-deployment reports

---

## Process Improvement Officer

### Role Summary
Process Improvement Officers facilitate retrospectives, identify process improvements, and drive continuous improvement initiatives based on lessons learned.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate retrospectives and gather team feedback
- Analyze process metrics and identify improvement opportunities
- Develop and implement process improvements
- Track lessons learned and document best practices
- Mentor teams on continuous improvement methodologies
- Monitor process effectiveness and adjust as needed

### Goals
- Continuously improve team effectiveness and efficiency
- Build a culture of learning and continuous improvement
- Reduce cycle time and improve team satisfaction

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **With Project Managers**: Partners post-project on retrospectives and process improvement recommendations
- **With Development Teams**: Gathers frontline feedback on process pain points and facilitates improvement initiatives
- **With All Teams**: Drives cross-functional process improvements affecting multiple areas

### Typical Communication
- Retrospective facilitation and action items
- Process improvement proposals and impact analysis
- Metrics dashboards and effectiveness reports

---

## Risk and Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
Risk and Compliance Leads monitor regulatory compliance, assess risks, and recommend mitigation strategies to protect the organization.

### Responsibilities
- Monitor regulatory and compliance requirements
- Conduct risk assessments throughout the project lifecycle
- Develop and implement risk mitigation strategies
- Ensure compliance with organizational policies and standards
- Provide risk and compliance guidance to project teams
- Track and report on risk and compliance status

### Goals
- Ensure organizational compliance with regulations
- Minimize business and operational risks
- Protect the organization from legal and compliance issues

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **With Project Managers**: Advises on compliance requirements and risk management strategies, reviews project plans for compliance gaps
- **With Technical Architects**: Collaborates on security and compliance architecture decisions
- **With Developers**: Provides guidance on secure coding practices and compliance requirements
- **With Stakeholders**: Reports on risk and compliance status and recommendations

### Typical Communication
- Risk registers and mitigation plans
- Compliance assessments and audit reports
- Policy and procedure documentation
